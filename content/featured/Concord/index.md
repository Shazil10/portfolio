**Built at Money Talks 2 · AI × Finance Hackathon (Money Operations track)**

<span class="featured-lede-name">Concord</span> is an AI agent that checks every trade before the money moves. It compares both confirmations against the live settlement instructions, runs 12 fixed checks, and decides Release or Hold. On a hold, it names the fault and urgency and drafts a note for a human to approve. The model only reads and writes; plain code makes the decision, which took it from 14/20 to 20/20 correct on the test trades.
